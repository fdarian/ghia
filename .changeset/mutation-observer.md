`MutationObserver` and `MutationRecord` are globals, with the standard's options, record fields and delivery (one microtask per batch, in creation order, `takeRecords`, `disconnect`, transient observers for a removed node). Both adapters report the nodes they place and remove, text changes (`characterData`) and prop changes, where an attribute is what `getAttribute` reads. With no observer alive each hook is one integer check. `queueChildListMutation`, `queueAttributeMutation`, `queueCharacterDataMutation` and `hasMutationObservers` are exported from `@ghia/native/host`.
