List markers. `li` is `display: list-item` and has a marker: a bullet (`disc`, `circle`, `square`, drawn as shapes sized by the font's ascent as Chrome draws them) or a number (`decimal`, `lower-alpha`, `upper-alpha`, `lower-roman`, `upper-roman`, with Chrome's counter text `1. `). `list-style-type`, `list-style-position` and the `list-style` shorthand are inherited properties; an `outside` marker ends at the item's border box on its first line and takes no part in layout, an `inside` one takes room at the start of the first line. The number is the item's place among the list items of its parent, from `start`, down with `reversed`, and set by an item's `value`; `ol start`, `ol reversed` and `li value` reach native from React and Solid (`forwardsCustomProp`). Inserting, removing or moving an item, or changing one of those or the type, renumbers the items after it in a retained frame. A list inside a list has no block margin and a `ul` is a `circle` inside one list and a `square` inside two, as in Chrome. Painted against headless Chrome's ink at scale 1 and 2 (`list-markers` oracle, React and Solid), and Lexical's list commands now show their bullets and numbers. Not supported: `::marker` styling, `list-style-image`, `counter-*`, `@counter-style`; the counter's scope is the parent element (`docs/fork/deviations.md`).
