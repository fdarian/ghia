A scroll no longer moves the hover under a pointer that stays, as in Chrome. `:hover`, `style.hover` and `pointerover`/`pointerenter`/`mouseover`/`mouseenter` (and their out and leave twins) stay on the element the pointer last moved onto while a wheel runs, and follow to what is under it 500 ms after the last tick of a mouse wheel, 100 ms after a trackpad lift (momentum that follows runs the 500 ms again), at the next frame after code scrolls an element by any path (`scrollTop = n`, `scrollTo`, `scrollIntoView`, `scrollToItem`, a key, a scrollbar; a smooth scroll when it ends), or at the next frame after a frame that changed the page (a commit, a restyle, a resize) or ran an animation of the layout (a transition or animation of a size, a margin, a position or the like, or a `layout` glide; a color, an opacity or a transform does not count, so `transition-colors` on rows and a spinner leave the hover where the wheel put it). A pointer that moves follows at once. Before, every wheel tick followed the hover, and the tick that did met the next wheel, so about every other frame of a live scroll over a `<virtual-list>` was built whole: a wheel under a still pointer is a translate now whatever hover the rows have (407 of 720 ticks were translates in the mailbox proxy with a live hover, 668 are). `getPatchCounters().scrollPatch` loses `hoverAsked` and `hoverMatters` and its `held` loses `HitboxChain`; `Hover` holds a frame for a pointer that moved only. A test that wheels and expects the row under the pointer to be hovered calls `advanceTime(500)` and draws the frames the follow asks for.
