HTML tags work: `<p>`, `<h1>` to `<h6>`, `<ul>`, `<ol>`, `<li>`, `<dl>`, `<section>`, `<nav>`, `<main>`, `<article>`, `<figure>`, `<blockquote>`, `<pre>`, `<hr>`, `<table>` and its parts, `<strong>`, `<em>`, `<a>`, `<br>`, `<button>` and the rest of the list in `docs/reference.md` ("HTML tags") are host elements with Chrome's default style (margins, sizes, weights, `display`), so markup written for the web looks and lays out as it does in Chrome. A tag whose default `display` is `inline` joins the paragraph of its parent like a `<span>`, a block tag ends it, and your own `style` or `<UniversalStyle>` wins over the default. The tags have their implicit accessibility roles (`button`, `link` for `<a href>`, `heading` with its level, landmarks, lists, tables), a `<button>` and an `<a href>` are tab stops without a `tabIndex`, Enter and Space click a focused `<button>` (Enter an `<a href>`) and Space on a button no longer scrolls. A `<label>` activates and names a `<button>`. In a form, a click on a `<button>` (`type` `submit`, the default, or `button`; `reset` does nothing) submits it with `event.submitter` set to the button, Enter in an `<input>` clicks the form's first submit button when it has one (a form with a submit button now submits on Enter whatever the number of `<input>`s), and `form.requestSubmit(submitter)` takes a submit `<button>` of the form (a `TypeError` otherwise, a `NotFoundError` when it belongs to another form). The JSX typings of `@ghia/react` and `@ghia/solid` know the tags, with `href` on `<a>` and `type` and `disabled` on `<button>`. Not done, listed in `docs/fork/deviations.md` ("HTML tags"): list markers, `<a>` link styling and navigation, `<dialog open>`, `<fieldset disabled>`, form reset and Chrome's themed button. `<code>` and `<select>` stay Ghia's own elements.
